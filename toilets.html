        Good to know

        Before you go

        • They're freeTfL's toilets cost nothing to use. A few run by other companies may charge.
        • Outside or inside the gates"Outside the gates" (TfL calls it the unpaid side) means anyone can use it. "Inside the gates" (the paid side) means you need to have touched in. "Outside the Tube gates" means it's in the National Rail station.
        • RADAR keySome accessible toilets are locked and open with a RADAR key from the National Key Scheme, sold by Disability Rights UK. If you don't have one, ask the station staff.
        • Changing PlacesLarger accessible toilets with a hoist and a changing bench, at King's Cross and Paddington on this page.
        • Plan a trip firstCheck a journey on the Journeys tab, then open this tab to see the toilets along the way.
        • Newest informationTfL keeps adding toilets. The TfL Go app now shows each toilet's location, whether it's inside the gates, its opening days and how to get in.
